## How the board works

1. A program is an issue with the label `program`; its page is linked from the issue.
2. A todo is a sub-issue of its program. Create one from the program issue (Create sub-issue) or with `gh issue create`, and it appears in Todo on the board.
3. Move the card across Todo, In Progress and In review as the work goes; a merged pull request that says `Closes #N` closes it and the board moves it to Done.
4. When the last open sub-issue of a program closes, the `program-progress` workflow closes the program, and the board shows it Done.
5. A meeting goes into the Meetings table of the program issue and of its page: the date, a rating from 1 (unsatisfied) to 5 (satisfied), and what was agreed.
6. Priority, Phase, Hours, Week and Deadline are fields on the board; the Hours view sums them per week and person against the 18 hours of the working pattern.
